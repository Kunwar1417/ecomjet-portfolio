What you want

The goal.

The brief is awareness: get AiSensy in front of a large, relevant audience. My audience is 344K people who follow me for AI tools, automation and running smarter businesses. A good chunk of them are founders and operators, the exact people who send customers WhatsApp messages every day.

Awareness tends to build on repetition. One reel gets seen; a short series gets remembered. That's the only reason there's more than one option below, so you can choose how far you want to take it.

I already use AiSensy myself.

I run AiSensy for my own skin clinic, WhatsApp reminders, booking confirmations and follow-ups with patients. So when it comes up on camera, it isn't a script read, it's me showing a tool I actually use. That tends to land better than an ad, because the audience can tell the difference.

My honest take

A quick note on the options.

A single reel works if you want to test the audience first, and that's a perfectly reasonable place to start. If awareness is the main goal though, a short series usually does more, since the same people seeing AiSensy two or three times in different use cases is what makes it stick.

That's why the per-campaign rate drops as the count goes up. No pressure either way, pick whichever fits your budget and how far you want to take this.
