What one campaign includes.

Every option is priced per campaign. A campaign isn't only a video, it's a full push with a clear next step for anyone who's interested:

01

The reel

One fully scripted, shot and edited Instagram reel built around a real AiSensy use case.

02

Story frames

A supporting story sequence to push the reel, add context and give a second touchpoint.

03

DM automation

A keyword-triggered DM flow so interested viewers get your link automatically, no manual replies.

04

Bio link

Your link featured in bio for the campaign window, so every profile visit has a clear next step.

The DM automation and bio link matter here specifically, since AiSensy lives on WhatsApp. The natural next step for an interested viewer is a message, and the campaign is built to route them there.
